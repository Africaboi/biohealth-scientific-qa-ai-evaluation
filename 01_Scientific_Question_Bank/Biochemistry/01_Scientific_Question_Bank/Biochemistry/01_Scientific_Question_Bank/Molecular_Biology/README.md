
# Molecular Biology Question Bank

## Overview

This section contains advanced Molecular Biology assessment questions designed to evaluate understanding of molecular mechanisms, gene expression, genetic regulation, molecular techniques, and the organisation and function of genetic material.

## Topics Covered

1. DNA Structure, Replication and Repair
2. RNA Transcription and Processing
3. Protein Synthesis and Translation
4. Gene Expression and Regulation
5. Molecular Genetics and Mutations
6. Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR)
7. Recombinant DNA Technology
8. Molecular Diagnostic Techniques
9. Epigenetics and Epigenetic Regulation
10. Molecular Mechanisms of Disease

## Assessment Approach

Questions will include:

- Advanced multiple-choice questions
- Mechanism-based scientific questions
- Interpretation of molecular biology experiments
- Application-based questions
- Molecular diagnostic scenarios
- Research-based scientific problems

Each question will include its correct answer, a detailed scientific explanation, an evaluation of alternative answers, and supporting scientific references.

## Objective

To demonstrate advanced knowledge of Molecular Biology and the ability to develop, evaluate, verify, and explain scientifically accurate information for AI model assessment.
