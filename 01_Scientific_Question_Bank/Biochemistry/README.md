# Biochemistry Question Bank

## Overview

This section contains advanced Biochemistry assessment questions designed to evaluate scientific knowledge, biochemical reasoning, and the interpretation of molecular and metabolic processes.

## Topics Covered

1. Enzymology and Enzyme Kinetics
2. Carbohydrate Metabolism
3. Lipid Metabolism
4. Protein Metabolism
5. Oxidative Stress and Antioxidant Defence
6. Bioenergetics and Cellular Respiration
7. Clinical Biochemistry
8. Biochemical Mechanisms of Disease

## Assessment Approach

Questions will include multiple-choice assessments, mechanism-based problems, experimental data interpretation, and research-based scientific scenarios.

Each question will be accompanied by its correct answer, detailed scientific explanation, evaluation of alternative answers, and supporting scientific references.

## Objective

To demonstrate advanced biochemical knowledge and the ability to develop, evaluate, and explain scientifically accurate responses for AI model assessment.
