# Molecular Biology Question Bank

## Overview

This section contains scientifically structured questions designed to assess knowledge and understanding of fundamental and advanced concepts in Molecular Biology.

The questions are intended to evaluate scientific reasoning, conceptual accuracy, interpretation of molecular processes, and the ability to explain biological mechanisms.

## Core Topics

1. DNA Structure, Replication and Repair
2. RNA Transcription and Processing
3. Protein Synthesis and Translation
4. Gene Expression and Regulation
5. Molecular Biology Techniques and Applications

## Question Format

Each topic will contain:

- Multiple-choice questions with one best answer.
- Clear explanations of correct answers.
- Explanations of why alternative options are incorrect.
- Scientific reasoning and relevant biological mechanisms.
- Quality assurance notes to identify possible ambiguities or misconceptions.
- Recommended textbooks and scientific references.

## Purpose

The question bank demonstrates the ability to develop scientifically accurate assessment questions, evaluate AI-generated scientific responses, identify factual errors, and provide evidence-based explanations.

## Academic Standard

Questions will be developed at intermediate and advanced undergraduate levels, with selected postgraduate-level concepts where appropriate.
