
# Biochemistry Question Bank

## Overview

This section contains scientifically structured Biochemistry questions designed to assess subject knowledge, scientific reasoning, critical thinking, and the ability to evaluate AI-generated scientific responses.

## Areas Covered

1. Enzymology and Enzyme Kinetics
2. Carbohydrate Metabolism
3. Lipid Metabolism
4. Protein Structure and Function
5. Bioenergetics and Oxidative Phosphorylation
6. Oxidative Stress and Antioxidant Defence
7. Clinical Biochemistry
8. Nucleic Acid Biochemistry

## Question Format

Each question set may include:

- Multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
- Correct answers and detailed scientific explanations.
- Evaluation of incorrect answer options.
- Relevant biochemical mechanisms and pathways.
- Supporting scientific references.

## Purpose

The purpose of this question bank is to demonstrate competence in developing high-quality scientific questions, evaluating AI-generated answers, identifying scientific inaccuracies, and providing evidence-based explanations.

## Quality Assurance Approach

Questions and answers are assessed based on:

- Scientific accuracy.
- Clarity and relevance.
- Logical reasoning.
- Completeness of explanations.
- Consistency with established scientific evidence.
- Reliability of supporting references.
