
# Molecular Biology AI Answer Evaluation

## 1. Introduction

This section presents the systematic evaluation of AI-generated answers to scientific questions in Molecular Biology.

It focuses on assessing the accuracy of explanations involving DNA structure and function, DNA replication and repair, RNA synthesis and processing, protein synthesis, gene expression, gene regulation, and molecular biology techniques.

The evaluation process demonstrates the ability to identify scientific errors, assess biological reasoning, verify molecular mechanisms, and provide scientifically accurate corrections supported by credible references.

## 2. Areas of Evaluation

The Molecular Biology evaluation section covers the following areas:

1. DNA Structure, Replication, and Repair
2. RNA Transcription and Processing
3. Protein Synthesis and Translation
4. Gene Expression and Regulation
5. Molecular Biology Techniques and Applications

## 3. Evaluation Objectives

The objectives are to:

- Assess the scientific accuracy of AI-generated molecular biology answers.
- Identify incorrect, incomplete, or misleading explanations of molecular processes.
- Evaluate the logical interpretation of molecular mechanisms.
- Examine the accuracy of explanations involving nucleic acids, proteins, and gene regulation.
- Verify scientific claims against authoritative textbooks and credible research publications.
- Provide corrected explanations using established molecular biology principles.
- Assess the clarity, completeness, and relevance of AI-generated responses.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

Each AI-generated answer will be assessed using six criteria:

| Criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| Scientific Accuracy | Correctness of molecular biology facts, mechanisms, and terminology. |
| Scientific Reasoning | Logical interpretation and explanation of molecular processes. |
| Completeness | Adequacy and depth of the response. |
| Clarity | Clear, coherent, and understandable presentation. |
| Evidence and References | Reliability and relevance of supporting scientific sources. |
| Relevance | Consistency with the question and its intended scope. |

## 5. Scoring System

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5.

- 5 – Excellent
- 4 – Good
- 3 – Satisfactory
- 2 – Poor
- 1 – Very Poor

**Maximum score: 30 points.**

### Overall Performance Classification

| Total Score | Classification |
|---|---|
| 26–30 | Excellent |
| 21–25 | Good |
| 16–20 | Satisfactory |
| 10–15 | Poor |
| 6–9 | Very Poor |

## 6. Standard Evaluation Procedure

Each evaluation will follow these steps:

1. Present a molecular biology question.
2. Provide an AI-generated answer sample, clearly identified as illustrative when it has not been obtained from an actual AI system.
3. Identify scientifically inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading statements.
4. Verify relevant claims using authoritative scientific sources.
5. Explain the identified errors.
6. Provide a corrected scientific answer.
7. Score the response using the six evaluation criteria.
8. Provide an overall assessment and recommendations for improvement.

## 7. Evidence and Reference Standards

Scientific claims will be checked against reliable sources, including:

- Alberts et al., *Molecular Biology of the Cell*.
- Watson et al., *Molecular Biology of the Gene*.
- Lodish et al., *Molecular Cell Biology*.
- Nelson and Cox, *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry*.
- Relevant peer-reviewed molecular biology publications.
- Reputable scientific databases and institutional research resources.

References will be selected according to their relevance to each evaluation topic.

## 8. Professional Standards

All evaluations will demonstrate:

- Objectivity and scientific integrity.
- Accurate interpretation of molecular mechanisms.
- Appropriate use of established scientific terminology.
- Clear distinction between established knowledge and emerging research.
- Reliable use of scientific references.
- Transparent identification of limitations in AI-generated answers.
- Responsible presentation of molecular biology methods and applications.

## 9. Scope

This section is restricted to Molecular Biology as one of the four scientific disciplines covered by this portfolio:

1. Biochemistry
2. Molecular Biology
