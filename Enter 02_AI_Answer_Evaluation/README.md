
# AI Answer Evaluation and Scientific Verification

## 1. Introduction

This section demonstrates the systematic evaluation of AI-generated scientific answers across four subject areas:

1. Microbiology
2. Molecular Biology
3. Biochemistry
4. Parasitology

The objective is to assess the scientific accuracy, reasoning, completeness, clarity, and reliability of AI-generated responses.

## 2. Objectives

The evaluation process aims to:

- Assess the factual accuracy of AI-generated scientific responses.
- Identify scientific errors, misconceptions, and unsupported claims.
- Evaluate scientific reasoning and interpretation.
- Compare alternative answers to scientific questions.
- Verify scientific claims using credible scientific literature.
- Develop accurate, evidence-based corrections and explanations.
- Assess the overall quality of AI-generated scientific content.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

Each AI-generated answer will be assessed using six criteria.

| Criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| Scientific Accuracy | Correctness of scientific facts, concepts, and terminology. |
| Scientific Reasoning | Logical consistency and appropriate application of scientific principles. |
| Completeness | Whether the answer addresses all essential aspects of the question. |
| Clarity | Whether the explanation is understandable and well structured. |
| Evidence and References | Whether important claims are supported by credible sources. |
| Relevance | Whether the answer directly addresses the question. |

## 4. Scoring System

Each criterion will be scored from 1 to 5.

| Score | Performance Level | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Very Poor | Major scientific errors or inadequate response. |
| 2 | Poor | Significant weaknesses and incomplete explanations. |
| 3 | Satisfactory | Generally acceptable but with identifiable limitations. |
| 4 | Good | Scientifically sound, clear, and sufficiently detailed. |
| 5 | Excellent | Highly accurate, comprehensive, well-reasoned, and evidence-supported. |

**Maximum total score: 30 points.**

### Overall Performance Classification

- 26–30: Excellent
- 21–25: Good
- 16–20: Satisfactory
- 10–15: Poor
- 6–9: Very Poor

## 5. Standard Evaluation Procedure

Each evaluation will follow these steps:

1. Identify the scientific question and expected learning outcome.
2. Obtain or prepare an AI-generated answer.
3. Examine the answer for scientific accuracy.
4. Assess its reasoning and completeness.
5. Identify errors, omissions, and unsupported statements.
6. Verify important claims using credible scientific references.
7. Provide a corrected, evidence-based answer.
8. Score the response using the evaluation criteria.
9. Document the final assessment and recommendations.

## 6. Documentation Format

Each evaluation case will contain:

- Scientific discipline and topic.
- Original scientific question.
- AI-generated answer.
- Scientific assessment.
- Identified errors or limitations.
- Evidence-based correction.
- Supporting references.
- Evaluation scores.
- Final assessment.

## 7. Evidence and Reference Sources

Priority will be given to:

- Peer-reviewed scientific publications.
- Established scientific textbooks.
- World Health Organization (WHO) publications.
- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) resources.
- National Institutes of Health (NIH) resources.
- Reputable academic and scientific institutions.

References will be selected according to their relevance, authority, and currency.

## 8. Professional Standards

All evaluations will follow these principles:

- Scientific objectivity and accuracy.
- Clear distinction between established evidence and uncertainty.
- Appropriate use of scientific terminology.
- Transparent identification of errors and limitations.
- Evidence-based correction of inaccurate claims.
- Proper acknowledgement and citation of supporting sources.

## 9. Portfolio Scope

The evaluation case studies will cover only these four disciplines:

1. Biochemistry
2. Molecular Biology
3. Microbiology
4. Parasitology

The completed portfolio will demonstrate scientific knowledge, critical thinking, research literacy, and the ability to assess the quality of AI-generated scientific information.

**Important:** Any AI-generated answers used as examples will be clearly identified as illustrative samples unless they have actually been obtained from an AI system.
