
# Protozoology

## Question 1

Which characteristic best describes protozoa?

A. They are multicellular organisms with differentiated tissues.

B. They are predominantly unicellular eukaryotic organisms.

C. They are prokaryotic organisms without membrane-bound organelles.

D. They are exclusively photosynthetic organisms.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Protozoa are predominantly unicellular eukaryotic organisms. They possess a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles, although their structural complexity varies considerably.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- A: Incorrect. This describes multicellular organisms with tissue differentiation.
- C: Incorrect. Protozoa are eukaryotes, not prokaryotes.
- D: Incorrect. Most medically important protozoa obtain nutrients through heterotrophic mechanisms.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Protozoa are a diverse biological grouping rather than a single formal taxonomic group.

## Question 2

Which structure is primarily responsible for locomotion in many amoeboid protozoa?

A. Cilia.

B. Flagella.

C. Pseudopodia.

D. Contractile vacuoles.

**Correct Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Amoeboid protozoa move through temporary extensions of their cytoplasm called pseudopodia. These structures also help capture food particles.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- A: Incorrect. Cilia are short, hair-like structures used for movement in some protozoa.
- B: Incorrect. Flagella are elongated structures used for locomotion by organisms such as trypanosomes.
- D: Incorrect. Contractile vacuoles primarily regulate water balance in certain protozoa.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Locomotory structures are useful descriptive characteristics, but they are not sufficient on their own for modern protozoan classification.

## Question 3

Which protozoan parasite is responsible for human African trypanosomiasis?

A. Giardia duodenalis.

B. Trypanosoma brucei.

C. Entamoeba histolytica.

D. Leishmania donovani.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Trypanosoma brucei causes human African trypanosomiasis, also known as sleeping sickness. The infection is transmitted by infected tsetse flies.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- A: Incorrect. Giardia duodenalis causes giardiasis, an intestinal infection.
- C: Incorrect. Entamoeba histolytica causes amoebiasis.
- D: Incorrect. Leishmania donovani is associated with visceral leishmaniasis.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Disease names, causative organisms and transmission routes should be correctly matched.

## Question 4

Which stage of Giardia duodenalis is primarily responsible for transmission between human hosts?

A. Trophozoite.

B. Cyst.

C. Merozoite.

D. Gametocyte.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** The cyst is the environmentally resistant infective stage of Giardia duodenalis. Infection commonly occurs through ingestion of cysts in contaminated food or water.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- A: Incorrect. Trophozoites are the active feeding and multiplying forms found in the intestinal tract.
- C: Incorrect. Merozoites are developmental stages associated with certain apicomplexan parasites.
- D: Incorrect. Gametocytes are sexual precursor stages found in some protozoan life cycles, including Plasmodium.

**Quality Assurance Note:** The infective stage should be distinguished from the active feeding or reproductive stage.

## Question 5

Which protozoan is the principal cause of amoebic dysentery?

A. Entamoeba coli.

B. Entamoeba histolytica.

C. Balantidium coli.

D. Trichomonas vaginalis.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Entamoeba histolytica causes amoebiasis and may produce intestinal disease characterised by abdominal discomfort and dysentery. It can also spread beyond the intestine, particularly to the liver.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- A: Incorrect. Entamoeba coli is generally considered a non-pathogenic intestinal amoeba.
- C: Incorrect. Balantidium coli causes balantidiasis, another intestinal protozoan infection.
- D: Incorrect. Trichomonas vaginalis causes trichomoniasis, a sexually transmitted infection.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Not all intestinal amoebae are pathogenic; accurate species identification is important.

## Question 6

Which organelle is characteristically associated with the energy metabolism of many trypanosomatid parasites?

A. Glycosome.

B. Chloroplast.

C. Lysosomal nucleus.

D. Cell wall.

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Glycosomes are specialised peroxisome-related organelles found in trypanosomatids. They contain several enzymes involved in glycolysis and related metabolic pathways.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- B: Incorrect. Chloroplasts are photosynthetic organelles and are not characteristic of trypanosomatid parasites.
- C: Incorrect. A lysosomal nucleus is not a recognised organelle.
- D: Incorrect. A cell wall is not an organelle and is not characteristic of trypanosomatids.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Glycosomes are an important example of specialised cellular organisation in kinetoplastid parasites.

## Question 7

Which statement correctly describes the life cycle of Plasmodium in humans?

A. Sexual reproduction occurs exclusively in human red blood cells.

B. Asexual multiplication occurs in humans, while sexual reproduction is completed in the female Anopheles mosquito.

C. The parasite reproduces only in the mosquito and does not multiply in humans.

D. Human infection occurs exclusively through ingestion of mature gametocytes.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Explanation:** Plasmodium undergoes asexual development in the human host, including liver-stage development and multiplication in red blood cells. Sexual development and fertilisation occur in the mosquito following ingestion of gametocytes.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- A: Incorrect. Red blood cell stages involve asexual multiplication, not sexual reproduction.
- C: Incorrect. Extensive asexual multiplication occurs in humans.
- D: Incorrect. Human infection usually follows inoculation of sporozoites by an infected female Anopheles mosquito.

**Quality Assurance Note:** The mosquito is the definitive host because sexual reproduction occurs in it.

## Question 8

Which protozoan parasite is associated with visceral leishmaniasis?

A. Leishmania donovani.

B. Trypanosoma cruzi.

C. Cryptosporidium parvum.

D. Toxoplasma gondii.

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Leishmania donovani is a major causative agent of visceral leishmaniasis, a disease that can affect internal organs, particularly the spleen, liver and bone marrow.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- B: Incorrect. Trypanosoma cruzi causes Chagas disease.
- C: Incorrect. Cryptosporidium parvum causes cryptosporidiosis.
- D: Incorrect. Toxoplasma gondii causes toxoplasmosis.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Leishmania species are associated with different clinical forms of leishmaniasis.

## Question 9

Which laboratory method is commonly used to detect Plasmodium parasites in peripheral blood?

A. Peripheral blood film microscopy.

B. Urine culture.

C. Sputum acid-fast staining.

D. Stool occult blood testing.

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Examination of stained thick and thin peripheral blood films is an established method for malaria diagnosis. Thick films improve parasite detection, while thin films assist with species identification and assessment of parasitaemia.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- B: Incorrect. Urine culture is primarily used to investigate urinary tract infections.
- C: Incorrect. Acid-fast staining of sputum is commonly associated with the investigation of tuberculosis.
- D: Incorrect. Occult blood testing detects hidden blood in stool and is not a malaria diagnostic method.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Diagnostic interpretation should consider microscopy quality, parasite density and the possibility of low-level parasitaemia.

## Question 10

Which protozoan parasite is commonly associated with opportunistic encephalitis in individuals with severe immunosuppression?

A. Toxoplasma gondii.

B. Giardia duodenalis.

C. Trichomonas vaginalis.

D. Entamoeba coli.

**Correct Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Toxoplasma gondii can cause toxoplasmic encephalitis, particularly in individuals with severe immunosuppression. Reactivation of latent infection is an important clinical mechanism.

**Distractor Evaluation:**
- B: Incorrect. Giardia duodenalis primarily causes intestinal disease.
- C: Incorrect. Trichomonas vaginalis causes urogenital infection.
- D: Incorrect. Entamoeba coli is generally a non-pathogenic intestinal amoeba.

**Quality Assurance Note:** Host immune status is an important determinant of the clinical presentation of opportunistic protozoan infections.
