
# Parasitology Question Bank

## Overview

This section contains scientifically accurate, evidence-based questions covering fundamental and advanced concepts in medical and biological parasitology.

The questions are designed to assess knowledge of parasite classification, morphology, life cycles, host–parasite relationships, pathogenesis, diagnosis, epidemiology, prevention and control.

## Core Topics

1. General Principles and Classification of Parasites
2. Protozoology
3. Helminthology
4. Medical Entomology and Vector Biology
5. Parasite Life Cycles and Host–Parasite Relationships
6. Parasite Pathogenesis and Host Immune Responses
7. Laboratory Diagnosis of Parasitic Infections
8. Parasite Epidemiology, Prevention and Control

## Question Format

Each topic will contain multiple-choice questions with:

- Four answer options.
- One best answer.
- Detailed explanations of correct answers.
- Evaluation of incorrect options.
- Scientific quality assurance notes.
- Relevant academic references.

## Purpose

This question bank demonstrates competence in scientific question development, biological knowledge assessment, evidence-based reasoning and evaluation of AI-generated scientific responses.

## Academic Standard

Questions will be developed at intermediate and advanced undergraduate levels, with appropriate postgraduate-level concepts where relevant.

Scientific accuracy, clarity, relevance and consistency with established parasitology literature will guide content development.
